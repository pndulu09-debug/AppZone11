Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dulu Store and AppZone ("we", "us", "our") collect, use and protect information when you use our Android app and website (together, the "Service"). By using the Service you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Free Fire UID and server that you enter to receive likes.
  • Device identifier: a one-way hashed identifier derived from your device. We use it to keep your coin balance, apply referral rules and prevent abuse. We cannot read your original device ID from the hash.
  • Coins and referral data: your coin balance, your referral code, and whether you used or shared a referral code.
  • Order and payment details: package, amount, order ID, order status and the UPI transaction ID (UTR) you submit. We never receive your bank, card or UPI PIN details.
  • Technical data: IP address and basic request information, used for security, fraud prevention and rate limiting.

2. How we use information

  • To deliver likes and process orders.
  • To credit coins for watching ads and referrals, and to let you redeem coins.
  • To verify payments, prevent fraud and enforce limits (for example, free likes once per day per UID).
  • To provide support and improve the Service.

3. Ads and third-party services

To earn coins you can open an ad page provided by a third-party link-monetization service (VPLink). That service may show ads and may use cookies or similar technologies under its own privacy policy. We do not control those third parties. We also use hosting and database providers (such as Vercel and Upstash) to run the Service, and Telegram for customer support.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us operate the Service, or when required by law or to protect our rights and prevent fraud.

5. Data retention

We keep order records, coin balances and referral data for as long as needed to provide the Service, resolve disputes and prevent abuse. Temporary security data (such as daily limits) expires automatically.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the data linked to your UID or device by contacting us. Some records (such as completed orders) may need to be kept for legal and fraud-prevention reasons.

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the effective date updated above.
